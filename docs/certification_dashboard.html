Graphics Engine Architecture

  • Floating-Origin Camera: Centers world on camera in local ENU; eliminates floating-point jitter across planetary distances.
  • Depth32Float Reversed-Z: Allocates logarithmic precision to near/mid-field, eliminating coplanar road deck Z-fighting.
  • WGSL PBR Shaders: Direct solar illumination, Cook-Torrance specular highlights, Rayleigh atmospheric scattering, distance fog.
  • Glassmorphic HUD: 2D screen-space overlay with 5x7 ASCII font, speedometer, compass heading, contract HUD, and live OS RSS.

Vehicle Ground Dynamics & Anti-Tunneling

Eliminates vehicle sinking into elevation grids using 4-wheel bilinear DEM surface normal sampling and non-linear bump-stop restoration:

  • • Bump-Stop Restorative Force: Non-linear progressive spring forces restore chassis immediately upon penetration.
  • • Safety Clamp: Absolute geometric clamp prevents frame-rate-drop tunneling through steep terrain.
